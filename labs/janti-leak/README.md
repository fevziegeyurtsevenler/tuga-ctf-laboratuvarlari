## 🇬🇧 English

### 🎭 Story
JANTİ is a rising indie-electronic band gearing up for their mainstream debut. For months, the band's member B. stayed locked away in a makeshift studio set up inside the dark basement of their house, pouring sleepless nights into crafting the lyrics and composition for their breakthrough single.

Studio production was finally wrapped, and the track was scheduled for official release in just three days. Then, the unthinkable happened: an unreleased pre-master cut was abruptly leaked onto the underground streaming hub Spotifly.

The leak caught the band completely off guard. Label investigators determined this was not an accident, but targeted cyber sabotage orchestrated by a rogue computer engineer affiliated with a rival music group.

Before vanishing, the intruder left subtle digital footprints inside the audio file leading to a restricted backstage portal where a surveillance snapshot of the suspect has surfaced. Follow the trail, trace the intrusion, and identify the engineer behind the breach.
